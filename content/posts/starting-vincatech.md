### Why Another Tech Blog?
I have been thinking about this little project for a while, and after the usual procrastination, I finally got started on it. With the help of my trusty assistant, ChatGPT, I got this site up and running in no time.

Like a lot of projects I start, they can sometimes get abandoned and forgotten about. Let's see what happens to this one.

### What Will Be Here Soon?

Hopefully, my ramblings with a bit of useful stuff thrown in. Mostly my homelab stuff and anything else I happen to be tinkering with.

I am predominantly a Linux and open-source user, so it will usually be tied in with that. I also love the Raspberry Pi ecosystem. I know they are expensive at the moment, but I live in a caravan (grey nomad), so being able to run stuff on 12V is a must.
